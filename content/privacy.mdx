# privacy policy.

your privacy is important. this website is a static portfolio and does not collect or process personal data, except:
- **direct inquiries**: if you reach out via email, your message is used solely to respond to your inquiry and is never sold, shared, or used for marketing.
- **third-party comments**: the blog comments section is powered by giscus, which stores data on github discussions. you can review their documentation on github.
- **cookies & trackers**: no tracking or advertising cookies are set by this website.

if you have any questions, you can contact me directly at businesswithderwins8a@duck.com.
