# about me.

i am a design-focused frontend developer based in venezuela, passionate about bridging the gap between engineering and user experience. i focus on creating clean, tactile interfaces that perform exceptionally well.

---

## what i do.

- **frontend engineering**: craft accessible, semantic, and performant web applications using react, next.js, typescript, and tailwind.
- **tactile interactions**: design fluid animations, micro-interactions, and state management that makes software feel alive and responsive.
- **design systems**: build highly structured design tokens and components that scale across large products and codebases.

---

## my philosophy.

i believe good design is invisible. it is about solving complex problems through simple, clear patterns. i like to reduce friction, eliminate unnecessary decoration, and make every element on a page earn its place. 

when i am not coding, i explore generative design, experiment with swiftui, read about typography, and drink dark roast coffee.

---

## tech stack.

- **languages**: javascript, typescript, swift, css, html
- **frameworks**: next.js, react, swiftui, node.js
- **styling**: tailwind css, vanillacss, postcss, oklch
- **tools**: git, figma, vscode, playwright
