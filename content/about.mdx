- **frontend engineering** — accessible, semantic, and performant web applications using react, next.js, typescript, and tailwind. i care about bundle size, core web vitals, and the last pixel.

## my philosophy.

> "details are not the details. they make the design." — charles eames

i believe great software is invisible. it removes friction, clarifies intent, and earns trust through consistency. every element on a page should earn its place.

i am obsessed with:
- **light** — how surfaces feel under it, how shadows create depth and hierarchy.
- **motion** — not decoration, but functional feedback that communicates state.
- **typography** — the rhythm, hierarchy, and texture of a great type system.
- **color** — oklch over hex. perceptually uniform palettes that actually work in dark mode.

when i am not coding, i explore generative design, experiment with visionos, read about typography, and drink dark roast coffee.
